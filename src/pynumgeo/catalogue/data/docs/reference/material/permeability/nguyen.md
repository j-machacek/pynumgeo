#Nguyen model

The input line for the generalized from of the exponential expression of Nguyen[@nguyen2006] takes the following form:
	
	*Relative permeability = Nguyen 
	 k^w_min, k^a_min, m

The material parameters are given in the following:

* $k^{w}_{min}$ and $k^{w}_{min}$ are the minimum relative permeability for the pore water and pore air phase, respectively.
* $m$ is the exponent. For $m=3$ the original relation of Nguyen is recovered.

Note that an exponential model for the relative permeability analogously to the [exponential hydraulic model](../hydraulic/exponential.md) can be achieved by choosing the Nguyen model with $m=1$.
